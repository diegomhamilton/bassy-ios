## V0.5 RELEASE GATE

V0.5 is complete when the app supports this workflow:

```text
Open session
       ↓
Choose or edit a tone preset
       ↓
Practice and record with backing track, loops, and metronome
       ↓
Export a recording or loop
       ↓
Inspect useful performance diagnostics
       ↓
Recover gracefully from common audio-route failures
```
